# Fixed-camera populated board, November 13

`frame-00.jpg` is an unannotated 1280×720 JPEG captured from the external
Jieli camera during the user-authorized live investigation. One frame is
versioned to reproduce calibration and detection failures; generated sequences
belong in ignored directories. The extra blue bodies are backup pieces. Their
values must be read from bands, never assigned from the inventory.

The four existing corner markings are green (top left and bottom right) and
white (top right and bottom left). White pieces/background touch the white
markings. A colour centroid alone is insufficient. Four-region matching must
be validated by independent, distributed socket contacts in both banks.

This capture is evidence for this PC/camera/framing only. It is not Raspberry
Pi testing, a resistance measurement, electrical polarity verification, or a
recording of insertions/removals. The full-board profile preserves the existing
bank names: C is the lower bank in the photograph, B the upper bank; published
row numbering is reversed within each bank.
