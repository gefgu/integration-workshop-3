The initial task used saved pictures only. The user subsequently authorized
live pictures, raw recording, service restarts and deployment to the bench.
The live measurements below apply only to this PC and this camera arrangement.
No Raspberry Pi execution or bundle preparation was performed.

## Measured evidence

In the first ten sampled raw frames, the original single strip decoded 220 Ω
in 9, 470 Ω in 10 and 1 kΩ in 6, with zero wrong values. The updated reader
decoded the first 35 consecutive raw frames as follows:

| Value | Correct | Unknown | Wrong |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 220 Ω | 35 | 0 | 0 |
| 470 Ω | 35 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 kΩ | 34 | 1 | 0 |

The old blue-area minimum was 1,900 rectified pixels; visible blue areas in a
lesson frame were about 1,812 for 220 Ω and 1,079 for 1 kΩ. Paired-end evidence
removed that candidate-generation failure without assigning a value by inventory.
The exact service replay then reached 30 consecutive correct outputs after
ten startup frames, with zero wrong resistor values. Raw files and diagnostics
are ignored under `hidden/live-diagnosis`; seven small lossless regression crops
and their source hashes are versioned under `fixtures/current/band-crops-2026-11-12`.

That saved-recording result did not establish general live reliability. In a
later live sample of 120 distinct outputs over 30.399 seconds:

| Value | Correct | Unknown | Missing | Wrong |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 220 Ω | 120 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 470 Ω | 93 | 23 | 4 | 0 |
| 1 kΩ | 112 | 4 | 4 | 0 |

All three were correct together in 91/120 outputs (75.8%), with a longest
correct streak of 68. Processing throughput in that sample ranged from 3.0 to
4.5 fps (mean 3.957). Other components were added during the session; the initial
four-piece scene is not valid ground truth for scoring the later whole board.
Do not infer reliable whole-board detection from these resistor-only counts.

PC replay timing at 1280×720, 40 frames, concurrent tests and live service:
mean `PipelineAtual.processa` 0.2654 s, median 0.1856 s, first/max frame 2.9147 s.
The two band-read calls averaged 0.3857 s separately. These are PC measurements,
not Pi measurements or estimates.

The USB failure was independently explained by the kernel log: disconnect at
09:35:46, reconnect at 09:35:47, then `/dev/video3`/`video4` replaced the previous
`video2`/`video3`. The persistent capture link is
`/dev/v4l/by-id/usb-Jieli_Technology_USB_Composite_Device-video-index0`.
The updated service was started with that link and returned a valid fresh
1280×720 frame. A physical unplug/replug test of automatic recovery is pending.

## Limits and pending checks

- The Nov 11 whole-board 30-frame target still has an explicit expected-failure
  regression; the original saved-picture definition of done is not complete.
- The determinism regression covers 20 in-process and two subprocess replays of
  one frame. It is not evidence that every fixture sequence meets the full
  determinism requirement.
- Exposure 1600 is provisional for this lighting. Remaining camera controls
  need calibration. USB identity/recovery does not repair cable or power faults.
- The camera has no unique serial number. With multiple identical cameras use
  the intended USB port's `by-path` link; see `bench/service/CAMERA.md`.
- Deliberately unplug/replug the camera and verify recovery without a service
  restart, cleared stale state and renewed calibration. This was tested with
  fakes, not deliberately exercised on the live USB device.
- Verify all six real smart positions and both-bank calibration. Mode remains
  unread. No position is taken as evidence that a module is present.
- Run camera-settings calibration, record at least 60 seconds on PC and Pi,
  score those recordings and confirm band reads on real consecutive frames at
  the Pi's native resolution. Do not prepare a Pi bundle before those checks.

Assumptions: only the user-confirmed 220 Ω, 470 Ω and 1 kΩ band patterns are
eligible; 470 Ω is yellow–violet–brown. Red/brown are grouped as warm features,
so full pattern geometry and black-band evidence remain essential. Published
row reversal is within each bank, with unchanged columns and bank names.
