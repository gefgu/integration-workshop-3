## Note on Raspberry Pi 3 B availability

The Raspberry Pi 3 Model B is discontinued in Brazilian retail. As of September 2026 it was out of
stock at every specialist store checked — [Curto Circuito](https://curtocircuito.com.br/raspberry-pi-3-modelo-b-mais.html)
(3 B+ listed at R$ 420,00, *esgotado*), [RoboCore](https://www.robocore.net/placa-raspberry-pi/raspberry-pi-3-model-b-plus),
[UsinaInfo](https://www.usinainfo.com.br/placa-raspberry-pi-489) and
[Eletrogate](https://www.eletrogate.com/raspberry-pi-3-model-b).

That leaves two routes:

- **Secondhand** ([OLX](https://www.olx.com.br/anuncios/raspberry-pi-3-b), Mercado Livre) — listings
  from roughly R$ 100 to R$ 210. Cheapest, but no invoice, which may be a problem for
  reimbursement.
- **New 3 B+ from a retailer** — around R$ 420 when back in stock; a
  [3 B+ listing on Amazon.com.br](https://www.amazon.com.br/Placa-Raspberry-Pi-Modelo-Plus/dp/B0BNJPL4MW)
  was reported in stock. The 3 B+ is pin- and software-compatible, so it is a drop-in substitute.

The R$ 200,00 in the table budgets the secondhand route. If an invoice is required, budget R$ 420,00
for a new 3 B+ instead.
