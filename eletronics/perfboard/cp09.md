# CP09 — Regulated rails

**Win:** The isolated final regulators produce their specified voltages.

Build the assigned functional groups in the [assembly app](tscircuit/README.md). Earlier soldered wiring stays fixed. Removable links and top-side kit arrangements may change.

## Link positions

- USB prototype source and prototype 3V3 links removed before final supplies connect.
- Use checkpoint-specific bounded test loads; stop and isolate power before changing any link.
