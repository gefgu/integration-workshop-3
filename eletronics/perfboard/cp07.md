# CP07 — Read real voltage

**Win:** The capsule reading follows the multimeter as loop voltage changes.

Build the assigned functional groups in the [assembly app](tscircuit/README.md). Earlier soldered wiring stays fixed. Removable links and top-side kit arrangements may change.

## Link positions

- Prototype source only: positive C2 / ground C1; final supply and matrix disconnected.
- OLED uses ESP1 GPIO4 SDA / GPIO16 SCL at 3.3V. Remove diagnostic probes before ordinary operation.

## Guided actions

1. **Disconnect power and prepare:** Unplug USB and all educational sources before construction. Prototype source only: positive C2 / ground C1; final supply and matrix disconnected. OLED uses ESP1 GPIO4 SDA / GPIO16 SCL at 3.3V. Remove diagnostic probes before ordinary operation.
2. **Check the firmware contract:** factory voltage: ESP1 OLED + local ADS1115 0x48; USB3V3 to isolated card supply via removable links; P3/ammeter disconnected
3. **Measure and record:** With prototype source and diagnostic probes isolated as specified, compare capsule V(P1)−V(P2) with DMM at zero and the actual limited-source voltage. Error ≤0.10V+5%|reading|; ≥10 updates/s. All node absolute voltages 0–5.25V.
4. **Film the win:** The capsule reading follows the multimeter as loop voltage changes.
5. **Accept this checkpoint:** Verify the readings and construction, then accept CP07.
