# CP01 — Reject an open contact

**Win:** A lifted jumper contact is electronically rejected.

Build the assigned functional groups in the [assembly app](tscircuit/README.md). Earlier soldered wiring stays fixed. Removable links and top-side kit arrangements may change.

## Link positions

- USB test-source positive lead unplugged; final supply and matrix leads absent.
- TEST_A→A1 C4, TEST_B→C1; JP_RETURN fitted only during diagnostic test.
- Before LED enable: stimulus OFF, JP_RETURN/probes removed, prototype positive restored with power OFF.
