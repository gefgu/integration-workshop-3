# CP14 — Cutoff timing and transients

**Win:** Measured cutoff timing and contact peaks meet the final limits.

Build the assigned functional groups in the [assembly app](tscircuit/README.md). Earlier soldered wiring stays fixed. Removable links and top-side kit arrangements may change.

## Guided actions

1. **Disconnect power and prepare:** Unplug USB and all educational sources before construction. USB prototype source and prototype 3V3 links removed before final supplies connect. Use checkpoint-specific bounded test loads; stop and isolate power before changing any link.
2. **Check the firmware contract:** factory final-power qualification: explicit enable/reset; supervised ALIVE link only before CP13; no production authorization
3. **Measure and record:** Scope current at a switched 100Ω load: source current cut off within 1ms, including frozen controller. Do not use UART timestamps as timing evidence.
4. **Measure and record:** Independent OV disconnects: sweep 5V/3V3 buck interfaces with consumers isolated; verify actual thresholds and strict contact peak ≤5.25V during a bounded 12V failed-buck step. Test both guards separately before connecting any IC.
5. **Film the win:** Measured cutoff timing and contact peaks meet the final limits.
6. **Accept this checkpoint:** Verify the readings and construction, then accept CP14.
