# Revision 7 — testable perfboards

**Candidate, not released for soldering.** Same four 10×20 cm boards, same functional parts, same nets and install phases as revision 6. What changes is how the two lower boards are wired and how easy they are to test. Revision 5 and 6 are untouched; this revision is generated by `design/revision7.mjs` through `design/model7.mjs`, `design/place7.mjs`, `design/testaids7.mjs` and `design/links7.mjs`.

Read [bringup-pass-tables.pdf](bringup-pass-tables.pdf) and [probe-and-test-points.pdf](probe-and-test-points.pdf) first, then [selftest.md](selftest.md). [assessment.pdf](assessment.pdf) compares revision 7 with revision 6. Drawings, wiring and checkpoint PDFs have the same layout as revision 6.

## What changed

| Change | Effect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Spatial wire chains.** Each net is chained in board order, nearest already-installed pin first. The phase rule is unchanged: a wire only joins pins that are already installed. | Shorter wires, fewer crossings. | `model7.mjs` `spatialTree`, `finish7` |
| **Ground bus bars (power and router-matrix).** Up to four bare bars on free rows of the underside; each pin gets one short insulated stub; bars of one net are tied together in install order. Every insulated wire that crosses a bar is flagged for a sleeve (wire CSV column "sleeve over bar wires"). | One cold joint cannot open ground downstream. Not a density gain (see below). | `model7.mjs` `addBars`, `flagSleeves` |
| **Probe header `J_PROBE` (1×8, row 1, top left).** Same pinout on both lower boards: `GND, 5V, 3V3, 12V, SIG1, SIG2, TRIG, FAULT`. | One probe position for every board. | `testaids7.mjs` |
| **Status LEDs (row 38).** Power: 12 V fused, 5 V buck, 3.3 V buck, 5 V system, 3.3 V system, no-fault. Router: 3.3 V router, 5 V, 12 V, `ROUTER_ALIVE`. | See rails and watchdog state without a meter. | `testaids7.mjs` |
| **Designated test pads.** Existing pads picked per net (`testpoints.csv`, ringed on the TOP/UNDERSIDE drawings). No extra part and no extra wire. | Labelled probe points with zero density cost. Analogue nodes are not brought out. | `testaids7.mjs` `designate` |
| **Seven removable links** (`links.csv`): power `JP_R7_ENABLE`, `JP_R7_ALIVE`, `JP_R7_PASS`; router `JP_R7_EN`, `JP_R7_STBA`, `JP_R7_STBB`, `JP_R7_RST`. | Test the interlock logic with the rail provably OFF; stop ESP1 enabling the rail; cut the matrix off its control. Every link opens to OFF. | `links7.mjs` |
| **Firmware self-test contract.** | One serial interface per ESP32, results as pasteable log lines. | [selftest.md](selftest.md) |

Links, bars, LEDs and the probe header add no original net: with every link fitted the netlist is revision 6's. The assessor merges `NET__B` back into `NET` before comparing the 1,659 pins.

## Measured results (generated, not bench evidence)

| Board | Crossings r6 → r7 | Wire length r6 → r7 | Bare bar segments | Wires needing a sleeve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| contact-a | 49 → 28 | 3.09 → 2.76 m | 0 | 2 |
| contact-b | 23 → 32 | 3.08 → 2.75 m | 0 | 0 |
| power | 854 → 608 | 8.56 → 7.75 m | 47 | 54 |
| router-matrix | 2,259 → 1,623 | 14.17 → 12.10 m | 51 | 65 |

Ceilings enforced by the assessor: contact 60, power 700, router-matrix 1,800 crossings. Tighten them as the layout improves.

**Why bars are ground-only and not a crossing win.** A sweep over bar configurations (power / router crossings): plain spatial chains 436 / 1,347; four ground bars 450 / 1,385; ground bars with every rail on bars 537 / 1,399. Bars do not reduce crossings in this placement because the placer is not rail-aware; they are kept for ground because a daisy chain makes ground fragile. The remaining crossing increase over plain chains (about 170 and 240) is the cost of the probe header, LEDs and links, which sit at the board edges.

A rail-aware placer (parts in bands with a rail row between them) is the next step if density matters more than test access.

## Decisions and non-decisions

- **The student-rail trip stays discrete.** CP12 requires a trip above about 10.6 mA and at most 12 mA. That bound comes from the MT8816 analog-pin rating and the shared-Y-bus sums (ENFR6, EFR3, `guidelines.md`), and from the smart-channel clamps. Ready-made eFuse modules trip at hundreds of mA, so they would need a matrix and clamp redesign. Not done.
- **Candidate simplifications not taken:** fewer packages in the interlock (needs EFR9 and ENFR8 review), a ready OV/UV module for the LTC4365 stages (thresholds must match ENFR2), swapping the MT8816 pair or the TCA9517 buffers (EFR1, EFR22, EFR29). Each needs a part in hand, a bench trial and an equivalence sheet first.
- **No placement freeze yet.** Placement is regenerated deterministically; `revision7/placement.lock.json` is not implemented. Do not solder from these drawings until the physical grid, pogo and tray mock-up gates from revision 6 are measured.

## Regeneration

From the tscircuit folder: `npm run build:revision7`, or from the repository root `npm --prefix eletronics/perfboard/tscircuit run build:revision7`.

It builds native checks and drawings, checks pad copper, compares nets and phases with revision 5 (through revision 6's crosswalk), enforces the budgets, and writes the PDFs. Environment overrides for experiments only: `R7_BUS_NETS`, `R7_BUS_GND`, `R7_BUS_OTHER`, `R7_BUS_GAIN`, `R7_MAX_ATTACH`. Nothing generated is measured evidence; the revision 6 gates (fit, powered qualification, PCB manufacturing) still apply.

Connector MPNs stay in circuit data and are removed from schematic text by `strip-connector-mpns.mjs`.
