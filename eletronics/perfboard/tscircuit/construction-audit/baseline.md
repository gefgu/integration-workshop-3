Solder-operation estimate = installed part pins + two ends per prescribed wire segment. Several ends may share a joint; this is work accounting, not an exact count of unique solder joints. Excludes adapter soldering, socket pins, external modules, cable termination and strain relief; cable conductors include reserved NC conductors. Breadboard ties are excluded from wire-segment counts. No time estimate is inferred from these counts.

Most original permanent outlines exceed the selected 70×100 mm stock; the original A3/B3 outlines fit nominally. Component fit still needs review. Merely repacking does not prove a reduction in construction work.
