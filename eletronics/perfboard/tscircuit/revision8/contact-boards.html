Contact boards

Gold: contact solder runs. Colored: underside wires snapped to free holes. Underside view is mirrored. Bridge marks: insulated crossovers, no electrical connection; only GND/3V3 may cross other nets. Red = 3V3, black = GND, blue = SCL, yellow = SDA, green = NODE-to-matrix, purple = NODE-to-SMART (P1/P2/P3) leads. SMART branches leave the top of each NODE contact bus; NODE cable leads leave the bottom.
