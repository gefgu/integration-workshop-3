Revision 8 contact boards

Gold: contact solder runs. Colored: underside wires snapped to free holes. Underside view is mirrored. Bridge marks: insulated crossovers, no electrical connection; only GND/3V3 may cross other nets. Red is power; black is ground; blue, white, yellow and green are signals. SMART branches leave the top of each NODE contact bus; NODE cable leads leave the bottom.
