# Electronics guidelines

The authoritative perfboard rules are [perfboard/tscircuit/guidelines.md](perfboard/tscircuit/guidelines.md).

All hardware is assembled before CP00. CP00–CP09 change firmware and removable links only. Use numbered top and mirrored underside drawings for hand assembly, and pass the documented bench gates before continuing. See [the audit](perfboard/tscircuit/AUDIT.md) for design limits and verification status.
